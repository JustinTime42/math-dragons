Privacy Policy - Math Dragons

Last Updated: February 24, 2026

Welcome to Math Dragons, an educational math game designed to help children learn and practice mathematics in a fun, dragon-themed environment. We are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of children who use our app.

Important: Math Dragons is designed as a children's educational app. We take special care to comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

1. Information We Collect

Game Progress and Performance Data

We collect the following information to provide and improve the educational experience:

  • Math Performance Data: Your answers to math problems, accuracy rates, and progress through different difficulty levels
  • Game Progress: Which games you've played (Dragon Eggs, Fire Trail, Dragon Runes, Dragon's Feast), levels completed, and achievements earned
  • Virtual Currency: Dragon Scales earned and spent on in-game cosmetic items
  • Session Data: Time spent playing, gameplay patterns to optimize the learning experience

Authentication Information

We use Firebase Authentication to sync your progress across devices:

  • Anonymous Authentication: By default, we create an anonymous user ID to track your progress
  • Google Sign-In (Optional): If you choose to upgrade your account, we may collect your Google account email address for account recovery purposes only

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information such as name, address, or phone number
  • Camera, microphone, or location data
  • Social media information or user-generated content
  • Payment information (we do not currently offer in-app purchases)
  • Third-party analytics beyond what Firebase provides

2. How We Store and Use Your Information

Local Storage

Game data is stored locally on your device using Hive database technology. This includes your progress, settings, and earned Dragon Scales. This data remains on your device unless you choose to sync to the cloud.

Cloud Storage (Optional)

If you create an account, your game progress is synchronized using Google Cloud Firestore. This allows you to:

  • Access your progress on multiple devices
  • Restore your progress if you reinstall the app
  • Keep your math mastery data safe

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide personalized learning experiences based on your math skill level
  • Track your educational progress and adapt difficulty accordingly
  • Sync your progress across devices (if you choose cloud sync)
  • Improve the app's educational effectiveness

3. Third-Party Services

Math Dragons uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication: For secure account creation and management
  • Cloud Firestore: For cloud storage of game progress (Google Cloud Platform)

These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy Policy

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Math Dragons is designed for children of all ages. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • No Personal Information: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • Educational Purpose: All data collected is solely for educational purposes to improve the child's math learning experience
  • Parental Control: Parents can request deletion of their child's data by contacting us
  • No Marketing: We do not use children's information for marketing or advertising purposes
  • No Sharing: We do not sell or share children's information with third parties for commercial purposes
Parents: If you believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at the email address below, and we will delete such information.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • We use Google Cloud Platform's enterprise-grade security
  • Anonymous authentication minimizes exposure of personal data
  • Regular security updates and monitoring

6. Data Retention and Deletion

How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Local Data: Stored on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Cloud Data: Stored indefinitely to preserve your educational progress, but can be deleted upon request
  • Anonymous Accounts: May be automatically cleaned up after extended periods of inactivity

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • Local Data: Clear app data through your device settings or uninstall the app
  • Cloud Data: Contact us to request deletion of your cloud-stored progress
  • Account Deletion: We can permanently delete your anonymous or Google-linked account upon request

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify users of any material changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Providing notice within the app if changes significantly affect how we handle your information

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the information we have about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information
  • Data portability (receive a copy of your data)

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, your data, or Math Dragons, please contact us:

Email: privacy@mathdragonsgame.com

Subject Line: Please include "Math Dragons Privacy" in your subject line

For parents requesting deletion of a child's data, please include:

  • The child's approximate age
  • Any account information you may have
  • Specific data deletion requests
