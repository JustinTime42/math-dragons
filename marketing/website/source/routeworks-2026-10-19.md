Privacy Policy – Math Dragons | Routeworks Apps 
 Math Dragons / Privacy Policy Privacy Policy
 Effective date: February 15, 2026
 Last updated: February 15, 2026
 1. Introduction
 Routeworks LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) develops and operates the Math Dragons mobile application (“the App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially children.
 This policy applies to the Math Dragons app available on the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store.
 2. Information We Collect
 We do not collect any personal information. 
 The App stores the following data locally on your device to provide the game experience:
 Player profile information (dragon name chosen by the user)
 Game statistics (scores, play time, facts mastered)
 Math fact progress records
 App settings and preferences
 This data is stored entirely on your device using local storage. It is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. The App does not require an internet connection to function.
 3. Information We Do NOT Collect
 To be explicit, the App does not collect:
 Names, email addresses, or other personal identifiers
 Location data
 Device identifiers or advertising IDs
 Usage analytics or telemetry
 Crash reports
 Photos, contacts, or any other device data
 The App contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no crash reporting tools. The only third-party service is RevenueCat for managing in-app purchases, which receives only anonymous purchase data (see Section 10).
 4. How We Use Information
 The locally stored data described in Section 2 is used solely to:
 Track math fact mastery and personalize practice sessions
 Display game progress and statistics to the player
 Save user preferences and settings
 Maintain the dragon-raising game experience
 This data never leaves the device and is not used for any other purpose.
 5. Third-Party Sharing
 We do not share any data with third parties. Since no data is collected from your device, there is no data to share, sell, or transfer to any third party for any purpose.
 6. Children's Privacy
 Math Dragons is designed for children ages 5–12. We take children's privacy very seriously.
 The App complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable provisions of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding children's data.
 We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).
 The App does not require or encourage children to provide any personal information.
 The App contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no social features, and no external links that could lead children away from the App.
 The App does not connect to the internet and cannot transmit any information.
 If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at privacy@routeworks.app . Since the App does not collect or transmit data, we will work with you to address any concerns.
 7. Data Retention and Deletion
 All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all App data at any time by:
 Uninstalling the App (removes all data permanently)
 Clearing the App's data through your device's Settings > Apps > Math Dragons > Clear Data
 No data is stored on our servers, so there is no server-side data to retain or delete.
 8. Data Security
 App data is stored in the application's private storage directory on your device, which is protected by the operating system's application sandboxing. Only the Math Dragons app can access this data. No data is transmitted over any network.
 9. Advertising
 The App contains no advertising of any kind. There are no ad SDKs, no banner ads, no video ads, no interstitial ads, and no sponsored content.
 10. In-App Purchases
 The App offers optional in-app purchases to unlock additional content. Two full games are included free, and a one-time purchase unlocks all games, levels, and cosmetics. Optional consumable currency packs are also available.
 All purchases are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We use RevenueCat to manage purchase entitlements. RevenueCat receives only an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt data from the app stores — it does not collect names, emails, or other personal information.
 All purchases require passing a parental verification gate (a multiplication problem designed to be too difficult for young children) before proceeding to the payment flow.
 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy
 We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If we make material changes to how we handle personal information, we will provide notice through the App or through other appropriate means.
 We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
 12. Contact Us
 If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the App's data practices, please contact us:
 Email: privacy@routeworks.app

Support – Math Dragons | Routeworks Apps 
 Math Dragons / Support Math Dragons Support
 Need help with Math Dragons? Check the frequently asked questions below or contact us directly.
 Contact Us
 For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email us at:
 support@routeworks.app 
 We aim to respond within 48 hours.
 Frequently Asked Questions
 What age is Math Dragons designed for?
 Math Dragons is designed for kids ages 5–12. It covers addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division facts appropriate for elementary school students.
 Does Math Dragons require an internet connection?
 No. Math Dragons works completely offline. No internet connection is needed to play.
 Does the app contain ads? What about in-app purchases?
 Math Dragons has absolutely no ads and no sponsored content. The app is free to start with two full games (Dragon Runes and Fire Trail). An optional one-time purchase ($4.99) unlocks all four games, all levels, all dragon evolution stages, and all cosmetics. Optional Dragon Scales currency packs are also available. All purchases require passing a parental verification step first.
 How do I reset my child’s progress?
 You can reset all game data by going to your device's Settings > Apps > Math Dragons > Storage > Clear Data. This will remove all progress, statistics, and settings and return the app to its initial state.
 Is my child’s data safe?
 Yes. All data is stored locally on your device and is never sent anywhere. The app has no internet access, no analytics, and no tracking. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
 What devices and Android/iOS versions are supported?
 Math Dragons supports Android 6.0 (API level 23) and above, and iOS 14.0 and above. It works on phones and tablets.
 Can multiple kids use the app on the same device?
 The app currently supports a single player profile per device. If you need separate profiles, you can use separate user accounts on your device (if your device supports multiple users) or clear the app data between users.
 The app crashed or isn’t working correctly. What should I do?
 Try closing and reopening the app. If the problem persists, try restarting your device. If you continue to experience issues, please contact us at the email below with a description of the problem and your device model and OS version.
