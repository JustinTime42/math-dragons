# Algorithms and Craft: YouTube Shorts + Facebook Reels (2025-2026) for a Faceless Parent-Facing Math Channel

Research date: 2026-10-19. Source tiers are marked: **[OFFICIAL]** = platform docs or named platform staff; **[3P-DATA]** = third-party study with a stated dataset; **[3P]** = third-party blog/opinion; **[OLD]** = older than 2025. Several "2026" claims came via secondary coverage only and are flagged.

## 1. YouTube Shorts ranking signals (2025-2026), view-count changes, Shorts vs long-form

### Takeaway
Shorts distribution is a seed-audience test, then expansion or tapering based on whether viewers stop and watch (vs swipe), how long they watch, and engagement. YouTube publishes no weights or targets. Since March 31, 2025 public Shorts views count every start and replay. The old metric lives on as "engaged views", which is the one that drives monetization and the one worth tracking. In 2026 YouTube widened start-counting to all formats (Aug 24) and said it will cut Shorts reach for re-uploaded, non-original clips (Oct 1).

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Starting March 31, 2025, a Shorts view counts whenever a Short starts or replays, with no minimum watch time. The previous metric was renamed "engaged views" (Analytics, Advanced Mode). YPP eligibility and revenue still use engaged views. The stated reason was alignment with TikTok and Reels. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted); [Search Engine Journal](https://beta.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-changes-shorts-view-counts-no-change-to-monetization/543005/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/404562/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-shorts-views.html)
- [3P] Because loops now add views, passive replays inflate raw counts, so engaged views are the better comparison metric. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/how-to-analyze-youtube-shorts-performance)
- [OFFICIAL, via secondary coverage] Effective Aug 24, 2026 (announced around Aug 17-19, 2026), YouTube counts a view on play start across all formats (Shorts, long-form, live, podcasts). An engaged view is "clicking to watch or watching past the initial seconds". YPP earnings and eligibility are unchanged. — [Forbes, 2026-08-17](https://www.forbes.com/sites/gabrielalinzainescu/2026/08/17/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-views-handing-marketers-two-numbers-instead-of-one/); [YouTube Blog: What are engaged views](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/); [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2991785?hl=en); [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/325112/20260820/youtube-splits-its-view-count-two-bigger-public-numbers-same-earning-rules.htm). Agentio estimated public views would jump about 30%. — [Agentio](https://www.agentio.com/blog/youtube-view-vs-engaged-view)
- [OFFICIAL, via secondary coverage] Oct 1, 2026: a YouTube Help community post said the company is updating systems "to further prioritize original content and reduce the reach of content re-uploaded". The targets are channels that mainly aggregate or re-upload others' videos without significant changes. Commentary, editing, or storytelling counts as transformative. Minor technical or template edits don't. No start date and no dashboard indicator were given. Monetization still falls under the existing reused-content policy. — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/youtube-shorts-promoting-original-content-reposts-3718668/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/re-uploaded-shorts-lose-reach-as-youtube-favours-original-clips/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/418466/); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-original-content-update/)
- [OFFICIAL] YouTube Studio's Shorts analytics show "Shown in feed" (times the Short appeared in the Shorts feed) and "How many chose to view" ("the percentage of times that viewers viewed your Shorts versus swiped away"). This is the closest official hook metric. — [YouTube Help, quoted by OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [OFFICIAL, 2023 so OLD but still the most-cited] Todd Sherman (Shorts product lead), Creator Insider Q&A, Aug 2023: the system finds "a set of people that may enjoy your video", then grows or tapers reach based on their response. Other quotes: "The audience is the algorithm". A new creator's early views are "exploratory". Shorts views "mostly seem to come from the feed itself". No published target exists for viewed-vs-swiped. Creators should compare that figure across their own Shorts. — [OutlierKit transcript](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [3P] Other signals commonly cited: watch time and retention, likes, comments, and "Not Interested" feedback. YouTube hasn't published weights. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-shorts-algorithm); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [3P] Third-party tools commonly cite a "70-90% viewed" range for viewed-vs-swiped. This isn't an official YouTube target, so treat it as anecdotal. — [summary of vidIQ/Metricool/Buffer claims](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [OFFICIAL, via secondary] A May 6, 2025 YouTube blog post ("Debunking common myths about YouTube Shorts") says Shorts and long-form run separate recommendation systems, and YouTube has seen no evidence that Shorts hurt long-form performance. I couldn't open the original post. — [CreatiCalc summary](https://creaticalc.com/blog/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-long-form)
- [3P, unverified] A claim that YouTube "fully decoupled" the two systems in late 2025, so viral Shorts no longer surface long-form. No official source confirms it. — [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/guides/youtube-shorts-vs-long-form-strategy)
- [OFFICIAL] Since Oct 15, 2024, square or vertical uploads up to 3 minutes count as Shorts (previously 60s). — [YouTube Help, cited by OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [3P changelog, unverified against YouTube directly] Other 2026 changes: the dislike button was removed from the Shorts player (Jun 25, 2026), custom thumbnail uploads for Shorts arrived (Jul 2026), and a Shorts "series with seasons and episodes" feature was announced at Made on YouTube (Sep 23, 2026). — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)

### Inferences
- With raw views inflated by both changes, judge each Short on engaged views, the viewed-vs-swiped %, and average % viewed relative to its length. Don't use raw views.
- The Oct 2026 originality change matters for a gameplay channel. Raw Math Dragons gameplay is first-party footage, so it should be safe. Even so, each Short should carry its own narration, text, or story so it reads as authored work and not a template clip.
- A Shorts series feature, if confirmed, fits planned recurring formats such as "Math History in 60s, Ep. N".

### Gaps
- I couldn't fetch the original May 2025 "myths" blog post or the Oct 1, 2026 Help post. Both rest on secondary coverage.
- YouTube has never confirmed official weights or thresholds for viewed-vs-swiped, APV, or loops.

## 2. Facebook Reels ranking (Meta official), length, unification of FB video into Reels

### Takeaway
Meta's own Facebook Reels system card (updated Jun 19, 2026) is the best primary source. Ranking predicts watch time, watch-vs-skip, completion, likes, continued session, and negative feedback (hide or "see less"). Strong item-level signals include how often others watched the reel to completion and how long others typically watched it. Since mid-2025 all Facebook video is "Reels" with no length cap. From July 2025 into 2026 Meta has pushed reach toward original content and away from reposts and lightly edited content.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] The Facebook Reels system card (updated Jun 19, 2026) lists these predictions: how long you'll watch; whether you'll keep watching reels after opening one full-screen; whether you'll click a reel in Feed to go full-screen; likes; watch vs skip; watch to completion; ask to see less; hide; start a viewing session vs leave right away; move on to the next reel. — [Meta Transparency Center, FB Reels](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/)
- [OFFICIAL] Signals cited most often on that card: reels you recently liked, watched, shared, or commented on; the reel's author and authors you've watched; how many times others watched the reel to completion; how many times others viewed it; the reel's length; how long others typically watched it. Meta doesn't rank the signals by importance. — [Meta Transparency Center, FB Reels](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/); system-card background: [Meta AI blog, 2023](https://ai.meta.com/blog/how-ai-powers-experiences-facebook-instagram-system-cards/)
- [OFFICIAL, Instagram not Facebook] Adam Mosseri (Jan 2025) named watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach as the top three signals: "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/). Mosseri runs Instagram, not Facebook. Treat this as a strong hint for FB Reels (same Meta stack), not a confirmed FB rule. The FB card above doesn't list shares as a prediction, only as an input signal.
- [OFFICIAL, via press] June 2025: Meta said all Facebook video will be Reels regardless of length or format. There's no upper length limit (before the change FB Reels capped at 90s), and the Video tab became the Reels tab. Rollout was gradual over the following months. Instagram Reels still cap at 3 minutes. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/facebook-videos-are-all-just-going-to-be-reels-now-185807805.html); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/6/facebook-merges-all-videos-into-reels--drops-format-limits-and-unifies-sharing)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] July 2025: Facebook's update penalizing "unoriginal" content. Meta acted on about 500,000 spam or fake-engagement accounts in H1 2025. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-adds-more-measures-to-ensure-original-creators-get-credit/814764/); [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/facebook-updates-originality-guidelines-adds-impersonation-detection-tools-for-creators/)
- [OFFICIAL, via press, Mar 2026] Meta says views and watch time for original Reels on Facebook roughly doubled in H2 2025 vs H2 2024, which it attributes to deprioritizing unoriginal content. Original means footage you film or produce yourself. Remixes qualify if they add information, analysis, or story. Deprioritized: re-uploads, reaction videos without real commentary, stitch compilations, and minor edits (borders, captions, speed changes). Accounts that mostly post unoriginal content can become non-recommendable and be demonetized. — [TechBuzz](https://techbuzz.ai/articles/meta-doubles-reels-views-with-new-creator-protection-tools); [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/meta-cracks-down-on-spam-content-copycats-and-impersonators-on-facebook-reels/); [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/meta-pushes-facebook-to-prioritize-original-creators-crack-down-on-copycats/). This is Meta's self-reported figure, not independently verified.
- [OFFICIAL, Instagram, OLD 2022 / reaffirmed 2025] Mosseri said Instagram would prioritize original content in rankings. — [NBC](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/instagram-head-mosseri-says-app-will-prioritize-original-content-with-potential-tiktok-ban-days-away/6105247/)
- [3P] On ideal FB Reels length, practitioners disagree: 15-45s, 30-45s for tutorials, up to about 60s for narrative with "hooks every 10 seconds", and 90s treated as a practical ceiling. — [OpusClip blog](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-facebook-reels-length-format-retention); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/?p=119641)

### Inferences
- On FB, "watch vs skip", "completion", and "others' completion count" are explicit predictions and signals, so shorter, tightly looping pieces have a structural advantage for reach. Longer pieces (60-180s) suit parent-tip explainers once the account has an audience.
- The "keeps watching reels after opening one" and "starts a viewing session" predictions suggest Meta rewards reels that lead into more viewing. A recognizable series likely helps.
- A faceless channel needs to be visibly authored to pass FB originality: voiceover, custom animation, own gameplay, own scripts. Stock-clip-plus-captions compilations are explicitly at risk.

### Gaps
- I found no Facebook-specific statement from a Meta exec (Tom Alison or Facebook head) ranking shares vs watch time. The Mosseri statements are Instagram-only.
- I couldn't fetch Meta's own help page for current FB Reels length or ideal duration guidance.

## 3. Hook science and retention benchmarks

### Takeaway
The first 1-3 seconds decide the swipe. Platforms expose this directly (YouTube's viewed-vs-swiped, FB's watch-vs-skip prediction). Hard, sourced benchmarks are scarce. The best available is a third-party dataset putting the median Short around 45% average view-through and the top 10% around 91%. Specific "3-second hold" thresholds (e.g. "below 70% kills reach") are unsourced folklore.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] FB Reels predicts "how likely you are to watch a reel rather than skip past it" and completion. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/)
- [OFFICIAL] YouTube's closest hook metric is "chose to view vs swiped away", with no official target. — [OutlierKit quoting YouTube Help](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [3P-DATA] Quso.ai (508K posts, 1,900+ creators): median short-form average view-through about 45%, bottom 90% about 40%, top 10% about 91%. — [Quso.ai benchmarks](https://quso.ai/research/video-retention-benchmarks)
- [3P] Tiered framework: 50-64% APV average, 65-79% strong, 80%+ viral or top-performing. Under-15s clips should target above 65% (80%+ exceptional). For 30-60s Shorts, completion above 30% is strong. — [GoFaceless summary of ClipIQ/Retensis](https://www.gofaceless.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-rate-benchmarks)
- [3P] Shorts with 1M+ views average about 76% retention of their length. — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/how-to-analyze-youtube-shorts-performance)
- [3P, single channel] vidIQ's own Shorts average 73.6% APV (trailing 12 months, Jun 2026). — [vidIQ retention post](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/increase-audience-retention-youtube/)
- [3P] APV can exceed 100% on looping Shorts because rewatches add watch time. — [Shortimize retention](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-rate)
- [3P, unverified] "On-screen text hooks have 30% higher hook rates", attributed to Social Media Examiner but not verified. "Below 70% 3-second retention kills reach" names no study. — [PostEverywhere glossary](https://posteverywhere.ai/social-media-terms/hook-rate); [blog](https://data-reel-maker.lovable.app/blog/first-three-seconds-data-video-hook)
- [3P] FB hook window is said to be about 3-5s vs 1-3s on Instagram. Low-authority source. — [search summary of FB Reels guides](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-facebook-reels-length-format-retention)
- [3P] Hook guidance for Facebook: open with a visual or provocative statement in the first 3 seconds. — [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-facebook-reels-length-format-retention)

### Inferences (craft synthesis; practitioner consensus, not platform-confirmed)
- Stack visual, text, and verbal hooks in the first second, since many viewers have sound off (section 4). For a faceless math channel:
  - **Visual pattern interrupt:** a dragon breathing fire on a wrong answer, or a number transforming.
  - **Text hook:** a parent-facing claim or question in large type within frame 1, e.g. "Your kid isn't 'bad at math.' Try this."
  - **Verbal hook:** the first spoken line restates or extends the text.
- Open-loop or curiosity-gap formulas that suit parents:
  - "The trick Japanese 2nd graders learn that US kids don't"
  - "This is why your 7-year-old counts on fingers (and why that's fine)"
  - "Can you solve this before your kid does?"
  - "The 1,000-year-old reason we write numbers this way"
  - Payoff should come at or just before the loop point.
- Avoid a slow logo or intro card. The first frame must already be the content.
- For Shorts, use average % viewed above about 65% (under 30s) and viewed-vs-swiped as the pass/fail gates for iterating hooks. Compare within your own channel, as YouTube recommends.

### Gaps
- I found no rigorous public dataset on 3-second hold rates or controlled comparisons of hook types (visual vs verbal vs text).
- I found no OpusClip-published hook study with methodology.

## 4. Length, looping, captions/muted viewing, audio, pacing

### Takeaway
For reach, short (about 11-30s) and loopable wins in the best available data. Longer Shorts (up to 3 min) and uncapped FB Reels suit depth and conversion once there's an audience. Captions or on-screen text are non-negotiable on Facebook. The famous "85% muted" figure is a 2016 publisher stat, but the direction still holds. Original audio is now favored by originality rules. Trending audio is optional.

### Cited Findings
- [3P-DATA] Quso.ai, 108,138 "matured" Shorts: median views peak at 11-20s (median 901 views), drop under 10s and past 30s. A clean loop raises the rewatch rate. — [Quso.ai Shorts length](https://quso.ai/research/youtube-shorts-length)
- [3P-DATA] Nate Black (via vidIQ), 35 billion Shorts views: about 13s or about 60s tend to perform best. vidIQ says there's no single ideal length. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [3P, unverified] Vidooly, 2M+ Shorts: 40-60s had the highest average views, second-hand only. This conflicts with Quso. The difference is likely era (pre/post Mar 2025 counting) and sample. — [Argil.ai](https://www.argil.ai/blog/how-long-can-youtube-shorts-be)
- [OFFICIAL, 2023 OLD] Sherman: "To what length do you need to tell your story?" YouTube gives no ideal length. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [OFFICIAL] On FB, reel length is an explicit ranking input, and completion counts matter. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/)
- [3P, OLD 2016] Digiday: publishers reported up to 85% of Facebook video views with sound off (Mic: 85% of 30s views; PopSugar 50-80%). This is publisher analytics, not a study. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/silent-world-facebook-video/)
- [OFFICIAL, OLD] Facebook: "41 percent of videos were basically meaningless without sound" (study of FB video ads), and captions raised view time about 12% (attribution disputed). — [Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/?p=8580); [3Play Media](https://www.3playmedia.com/blog/captions-increase-viewership-for-facebook-video-ads/)
- [3P-DATA, survey] 5,616 US consumers aged 18-54: 69% watch with sound off in public, 25% in private. 80% say they're more likely to finish a video with captions (Verizon Media/Publicis, 2019). — [Beey.io](https://www.beey.io/en/captions-increase-viewership-accessibility-and-reach-23486/)
- [3P] Over 80% of FB users watch Reels sound-off initially. Figures are inconsistent across sources and unreliable. — [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-facebook-reels-length-format-retention)
- [OFFICIAL] Meta's 2025-26 originality rules explicitly say adding captions alone to someone else's video doesn't make it original. — [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/meta-cracks-down-on-spam-content-copycats-and-impersonators-on-facebook-reels/)
- [3P] Original audio (a voiceover phrase or a distinctive SFX) can earn attribution when others reuse it. Use trending audio only when it fits, and keep music under the voice. — [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-facebook-reels-length-format-retention)

### Inferences
- **Length by goal:**
  - Reach and discovery: 11-30s, a single idea, loopable. Trivia, "solve this", and gameplay moments fit here.
  - Trust and conversion: 45-90s for parent tips and math-anxiety explainers.
  - Longer FB Reels or 3-minute Shorts: math-history stories, once the audience exists.
- **Looping technique:**
  - End mid-sentence into the opening line, or end on the same frame or question shown at the start.
  - Put the answer reveal at the last second so rewatching to check is natural.
  - Avoid end cards. Under the 2025 counting rules a loop adds a view, and on FB completion is a signal.
- **Captions:**
  - Burn in large, high-contrast captions inside the safe zone, clear of the bottom UI and right-side buttons.
  - Parents often watch in public or near sleeping kids, so the piece must work fully muted.
- **Audio:**
  - Use your own voiceover (AI or human) and Math Dragons' own music or SFX as a signature sound. This supports originality classification on both platforms.
- **Pacing:**
  - No sourced data on cut frequency was found. Practitioner consensus is a visual change every 1-3s and a "re-hook" about every 10s on 30-60s pieces (OpusClip guidance above).

### Gaps
- I found no sourced study on cut frequency or pacing vs retention.
- I found no 2025-26 primary data on muted-viewing share for FB Reels specifically.

## 5. Posting frequency, timing, consistency, series, cold start

### Takeaway
YouTube officially says time of day and daily upload quotas aren't optimized for. Each Short is tested on its own merits with a seed audience. Third-party data says more posts mean more total views but can dilute per-post performance. Practitioner data points to sustained volume (on the order of 100-200 Shorts) before channels see steady growth. Cold start is per video, not per channel, but early "exploratory" views are noisy.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, 2023 OLD] Sherman: time of day is "not something that we think about optimizing for". "There is nothing in our systems" that rewards hitting a daily upload threshold (e.g. 5 videos or 10 Shorts per day). Early views for a new creator are "exploratory". — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [3P] Explore/exploit model: each Short is shown to a small seed group and widened if they engage. If the seed doesn't engage, views stall. No threshold is published. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [3P] Channels with at least 200 Shorts tend to see steady view growth (no sample size given). — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [3P-DATA] Buffer (Oct 2025) studied TikTok posting frequency across 11.4M posts and 150K accounts. A secondary summary attributes cadence advice to Buffer: 1-2/week to stay active, 3-5/week to grow, 6-9+/week to maximize growth. That's TikTok, not Shorts or Reels, and unverified against Buffer's page. — [Blog du Modérateur](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/frequence-publication-ideale-tiktok/); [Spikerz](https://www.spikerz.com/blog/posting-frequency-compared-instagram-vs-tiktok-vs-youtube-in-2025); [Buffer data hub](https://buffer.com/resources/buffer-data/)
- [3P-DATA] Tubular Labs: Shorts were 57% of content uploaded by US creators and 88% of views in 2025. Higher frequency raises total views and engagement but may dilute per-video metrics. — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/report-short-form-content-dominates-as-cross-category-collaborations-drive-engagement/)
- [OFFICIAL] FB Reels uses "the author of the reel" and "the authors whose reels you've watched" as signals, so author-level history accumulates. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/)
- [3P changelog] YouTube Shorts series with seasons and episodes was announced Sep 23, 2026. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)

### Inferences
- A realistic cadence for a small team: 3-5 Shorts/Reels per week, held for 90+ days before judging the channel. Judge individual formats after about 10-15 posts each.
- Series formats give viewers a reason to binge, which helps FB's "keep watching / start a session" predictions and possibly YouTube's series feature. Candidates: "Dragon Math Minute", "Math Anxiety Myths", "History of Zero", and "Can You Beat a 2nd Grader?".
- Don't delete underperformers or re-post them immediately. Iterate the hook on a new version.

### Gaps
- No official statement exists on how many videos a channel needs before the system "learns" it. The 200-Shorts figure is anecdotal.
- No Facebook-specific posting-frequency data from Meta.
- Best posting times for a parent audience: no reliable Shorts or FB data found. YouTube says timing isn't optimized for.

## 6. Shareability psychology for parents

### Takeaway
Sends and shares are the main route to non-follower reach on Meta (per Mosseri, Instagram) and an input signal on FB Reels. Classic virality research says people share content that is practically useful, high-arousal (awe, amusement, anxiety), identity-signaling, and socially valuable. Parents' sharing motives skew toward support, advice, affection, and pride.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, Instagram] Sends matter slightly more than likes for reaching people who don't follow you ("unconnected content"). — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/)
- [OFFICIAL] FB Reels uses "reels you've recently ... shared" as a top-frequency signal. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/)
- [ACADEMIC, OLD 2012] Berger and Milkman, about 7,000 NYT articles: positive content is more viral than negative. High-arousal emotions (awe, anger, anxiety) boost sharing, and low-arousal ones (sadness) reduce it. Practically useful content is also shared more. — [Journal of Marketing Research](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1509/jmr.10.0353)
- [BOOK, OLD 2013] Berger's STEPPS framework: Social currency, Triggers, Emotion, Public, Practical value, Stories. — [Jonah Berger, *Contagious*](https://jonahberger.com/books/contagious/)
- [ACADEMIC] Parents share to express affection and pride and to get support from family and friends. Benefits include keeping in touch and getting parenting support and advice. Mothers do more of the sharing than fathers (102 US parent interviews). — [PMC sharenting study](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11290302/); [USENIX Security 2024](https://usenix.org/system/files/usenixsecurity24-ghafourian.pdf)
- [ACADEMIC] Parents choose platforms based on perceived audience. — [Rutgers](https://scholarship.libraries.rutgers.edu/esploro/outputs/conferenceProceeding/Managing-Childrens-Online-Identities-How-Parents/991031654942904646)

### Inferences (mapping STEPPS to Math Dragons content)
- **Practical value** (strongest for parents): "Try this at dinner tonight" techniques, e.g. number bonds or a making-10 trick. Every tip should be usable in under 5 minutes, so it's worth forwarding to a co-parent or teacher.
- **Social currency / identity:** content that lets a parent signal "I'm an engaged, calm parent", e.g. "Math anxiety is contagious, here's how to not pass it on."
- **Emotion (high-arousal positive):** awe from math history and beautiful patterns, amusement from a dragon failing hilariously, relief that "it's normal that your kid…". Use mild, constructive anxiety only, never fear-mongering.
- **Triggers:** tie content to recurring moments such as homework time, report cards, back-to-school, the car ride, or "Pi Day". These remind parents of the video later.
- **Explicit share prompts:** "Send this to the parent who does homework duty" or "Tag your kid's teacher". These match the "sends" mechanism. (The CTA strategy itself is covered by another researcher.)
- Puzzles parents can try on their kid ("Ask your 7-year-old this") create a social, offline moment and a reason to rewatch or share.

### Gaps
- I found no study specific to parents sharing educational short-form video, and no short-form-specific replication of Berger and Milkman.

## 7. Cross-posting the same video to YouTube Shorts and FB Reels

### Takeaway
Cross-posting your own original video is fine on both platforms. What gets penalized is other apps' watermarks and re-uploads of others' content. Export clean masters with no TikTok or YouTube marks. Your own logo is OK.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, Instagram, 2023-24] Mosseri: a Reel watermarked with your own brand logo is fine and won't hurt reach. Instagram avoids recommending Reels with other apps' logos. Since 2021, TikTok-watermarked Reels reach followers but are kept out of recommendations. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/)
- [OFFICIAL, OLD 2022] YouTube adds a watermark to Shorts downloaded from YouTube Studio, which discourages moving them to other apps. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/18/youtube-shorts-will-start-adding-watermarks-to-discourage-cross-platform-sharing/amp/)
- [OFFICIAL] FB and YouTube originality updates target re-uploads of *others'* content and minor-edit copies, not creators posting their own original footage. — [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/meta-cracks-down-on-spam-content-copycats-and-impersonators-on-facebook-reels/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/re-uploaded-shorts-lose-reach-as-youtube-favours-original-clips/)
- [3P, unverified] Claims of a 2026 rule penalizing accounts with "10+ reposts in 30 days". No primary source. — [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/avoid-content-duplication-penalties-cross-posting-2026)

### Inferences
- **Workflow:**
  - Render one clean master (no platform UI or watermarks) and upload it natively to each platform. Don't download a Short from YouTube Studio to re-post it, since that adds a YouTube watermark.
  - Tailor the first-frame text and caption copy per platform: FB parents may need a slightly longer hook window.
  - Use native captions or text where possible.
- Stagger posting by platform if you want to A/B test hooks. There's no evidence a same-day post on two platforms hurts either.
- Because originality systems are automated (fingerprinting), avoid putting the same gameplay footage in many near-identical Shorts. Vary the narration and angle.

### Gaps
- No official YouTube statement specifically on non-YouTube watermarks in Shorts ranking.
- No official FB statement on cross-posted-from-YouTube content beyond the general originality rules.

## 8. Key metrics and benchmark values

### Takeaway
The YouTube primary metrics are viewed-vs-swiped %, engaged views, average % viewed, and subs per 1K engaged views. For FB they're 3-second views (where shown), average watch time, completion or retention, shares (sends), and non-follower reach share. Only third-party benchmarks exist, and the platforms tell creators to benchmark against their own past content.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] YouTube Shorts analytics: Shown in feed, % chose to view vs swiped away, engaged views (Advanced Mode). YPP uses engaged views. — [OutlierKit/YouTube Help](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted)
- [OFFICIAL, Instagram] Track average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach. Treat as a proxy framework for FB. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/)
- [3P-DATA] APV: median about 45%, top 10% about 91%. — [Quso.ai](https://quso.ai/research/video-retention-benchmarks). Tiers: 50-64% average, 65-79% strong, 80%+ viral. — [GoFaceless](https://www.gofaceless.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-rate-benchmarks). 1M+ view Shorts average about 76%. — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/how-to-analyze-youtube-shorts-performance)
- [3P] Viewed-vs-swiped "70-90%" range is unofficial. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [3P-DATA] Median Short (11-20s) gets about 901 views in Quso's matured sample. This is a useful sanity baseline for a new channel. — [Quso.ai](https://quso.ai/research/youtube-shorts-length)
- [3P] Monetization note: one source says that from Feb 1, 2027, Shorts revenue requires 10M qualified views per 90 days. This isn't a direct YouTube quote and is unverified. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)

### Inferences
- **Suggested per-video dashboard:**
  - YouTube:
    - Viewed vs swiped (target: beat your channel median, then aim for 70%+)
    - APV relative to length (under 30s: 65%+; 30-60s: 50%+)
    - Engaged views
    - Subs gained per 1K engaged views
    - Shares
  - Facebook:
    - 3s or 1-minute views
    - Average watch time vs length
    - Shares per reach
    - Follower vs non-follower reach split
    - Follows gained
- Because raw YouTube views now count every start (Shorts since Mar 2025, all formats since Aug 2026), any benchmark from before 2025 that uses "views" isn't comparable.

### Gaps
- No official benchmark values from either platform.
- No reliable FB Reels-specific retention or share-rate benchmarks from a large disclosed dataset.
