# MD-003: Einstein didn't fail math

## Concept
Parents get the real story behind "Einstein failed math": top grades in algebra and geometry, a 1935 Ripley's clipping he answered himself, and the true inconvenient part (at 16 he failed the general part of a Zurich entrance exam, then earned those sixes at his next school).

- A: Frame one: still Tallyclaw (unimpressed pose) beside a scroll stamped "FAILED MATH". On-screen text: "Einstein failed math?" plus Tallyclaw's speech card "I was there. He did not." Narrator: "Parents, Einstein failed math? Tallyclaw was there. His verdict: he did not."
- B: Frame one: a recreated school certificate in our own style with "Algebra: 6" and "Geometry: 6" glowing. On-screen text: "Einstein's math grades". Narrator: "Parents, the man who supposedly failed math got the top grade in algebra."
