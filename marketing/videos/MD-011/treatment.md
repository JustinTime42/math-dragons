# MD-011: Math-anxious parents: change how you help

## Concept
A parent who feels anxious about math gets one calm step (keep homework help short and calm, and add playful math that isn't homework) and the research behind it, at its real confidence level.

## Hooks
- A: First frame: an adult's hands resting over a worksheet (our own layout), a pencil set down. On-screen text: "Math stresses you out?" VO (Narrator): "Parents, if math stresses you out, this one's for you."
- B: First frame: the same hands sliding the worksheet aside and picking up a slice of a (drawn) pizza. On-screen text: "Anxious about math? Keep helping." VO (Narrator): "Parents, if you're anxious about math, keep helping. Just help differently."
