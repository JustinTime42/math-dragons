# Idea Bank

**Date:** 2026-10-26.

**How it was built.** Ideas were drafted per pillar against the four-pillar mix (`content-strategy.md` §3), the eight formats and their beats (`format-library.md`), the series list (§4) and the calendar hooks (§8). Every fact comes from the playbook's verified trivia bank (Appendix A), its evidence table, Appendix B (myths, used only as debunks), the research notes, the adaptive-practice spec, or the app's code. Every puzzle and trick answer was computed in Python. Each idea then went through the rubric's five gates; the ones that failed are listed under *Dropped at the gates*. Good ideas that would have pushed pillar 3 or pillar 2 past their share are listed, unscored, under *Reserve*.

**Scores are a first pass.** One agent scored everything, so expect bias toward its own ideas. An independent reviewer should check the gates, sources and scores. After that, Justin's picks re-score the bank: what he picks (and why) is the calibration signal for the next round. Totals use the rubric weights H×3, S×3, A×2, E×2, P×2, F×1, Fit×1 (max 70); tiers are A 53+, B 42-52, C under 42.

**Reading the table**

- **Scores** are H / S / A / E / P / F / Fit: Hook, Share value, Accuracy confidence, Our edge, Production effort (higher = easier), Funnel fit, Format fit.
- **Src** codes: `A#n` = playbook Appendix A item n; `AppB` = Appendix B myth row; `Ev: <row>` = a row of the playbook's evidence table ("The evidence supports..."); `MLE` = `research_notes/.../math_learning_evidence.md`; `py` = arithmetic computed in Python; `code:` = a path in `lib/`; `spec` = `docs/specs/adaptive-practice.md`.
- **Puzzles** state the answer (Ans), the common wrong answer, and every assumption. Where no source documents which wrong answer is common, the cell says "tempting wrong" and part 2 must not claim "most people".
- **Tags:** the rubric's dependency tags (`needs:tallyclaw-puppet`, `needs:adaptive-P#`, `needs:gameplay-capture`, `calendar:`) plus these helper tags: `still-art-ok` (works with still Tallyclaw art and the Narrator, no puppet), `needs:fresh-source` (a claim must be sourced before scripting), `needs:site-live` (needs the join flow live at playmathdragons.com), `needs:justin-story` / `needs:tester-story` (a true story only Justin or a tester can supply), and `recheck-before-publish` (volatile fact).
- **Adaptivity:** ideas tagged `needs:adaptive-P#` may be scripted now but publish only after that phase ships and its claim tests pass (spec §0, §2.1), using the claim wording verbatim.
- **Spoken CTA** appears only in pillar 4 ideas, after the payoff. Value videos carry the corner mark only.
