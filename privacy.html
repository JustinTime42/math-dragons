Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 15, 2026 · Last Updated: February 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Routeworks LLC develops and operates the Math Dragons mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. We are committed to protecting user privacy, especially that of children. This policy applies to all versions of Math Dragons available on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Dragon name (user-chosen)
  • Game statistics (scores, playtime, facts mastered)
  • Math fact progress records
  • App settings and preferences

This data remains entirely on your device using local storage. It is never transmitted to any server or third party. The App functions fully without an internet connection.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or personal identifiers
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports
  • Photos, contacts, or other device data

No analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting tools are included in the App. RevenueCat is the only third-party service integrated, and it handles only anonymous purchase receipt data.

4. How We Use Data

Data stored locally on your device is used solely to:

  • Track math fact mastery and personalize practice sessions
  • Display game progress and statistics
  • Save user preferences and settings
  • Maintain the dragon-raising game experience

No data ever leaves your device.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect data from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Math Dragons is designed for children ages 5-12. We take children's privacy very seriously. The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable GDPR provisions regarding children's data.

  • No personal information is knowingly collected from children under 13
  • There is no requirement for children to provide any personal information
  • The App contains no advertising, no social features, and no external links
  • The App cannot connect to the internet or transmit information

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at privacy@routeworks.app.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all App data by:

  • Uninstalling the App (removes all data permanently)
  • Clearing data via your device's Settings > Apps > Math Dragons > Clear Data

No server-side data exists to retain or delete.

8. Data Security

App data is stored in the application's private storage directory, protected by operating system application sandboxing. Only Math Dragons can access this data. No data is transmitted over any network.

9. Advertising

The App contains no advertising whatsoever. There are no ad SDKs, banner ads, video ads, interstitial ads, or sponsored content of any kind.

10. In-App Purchases

Two games are available for free. A one-time purchase unlocks all games, levels, and cosmetic items. Optional currency packs are also available.

All purchases are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. RevenueCat is used to manage purchase entitlements and receives only anonymous app user IDs and purchase receipt data — never names, emails, or personal information.

All purchases require parental verification through a multiplication problem that is difficult for young children to solve.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated through the App or other appropriate means.
