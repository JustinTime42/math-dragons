Built by a dad, for his daughter

My daughter was struggling with math facts. So I did what every parent does — I searched for an app. I found dozens. They all had the same problems: aggressive ads interrupting every third problem, subscriptions for basic features, hollow "gamification" that wore off in a day, and sketchy data collection from apps meant for six-year-olds.

So I built my own. Math Dragons started as a weekend project on our kitchen table in Alaska. My daughter named the first dragon. She'd come home from school and ask to play — not because I told her to, but because she wanted to see her dragon evolve. The math facts came along for the ride.

Now I'm opening it up to other families. It's still the same app — no ads, no tracking, no accounts required. Just four genuinely fun math games wrapped in a dragon world that kids actually care about.

Four games, one adventure

Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division for ages 5-9. Each game teaches math facts differently so practice never feels repetitive.

Dragon Eggs

Tap falling eggs to build equations before they pile up. Quick reflexes and quick math help baby dragons hatch.

Dragon Runes

Drag across mystical rune stones to build equations and cast dragon spells. 50 levels of puzzle-based math.

Dragon's Feast

Navigate a grid to munch the right numbers — multiples, primes, factors, and more. Dodge enemies, grab power-ups.

Frequently asked questions

What devices does it work on?

Android right now (phones and tablets). iOS is planned but doesn't have a timeline yet. You need a Google account to join the testing track on Google Play.

What age or grade level is this for?

Ages 5-9 (roughly kindergarten through 3rd grade). The adaptive difficulty system adjusts to your child's level, so younger kids get simpler problems and older kids get more challenging ones.

Is it really free? Will it stay free?

It's 100% free right now — all four games, all levels, everything. If you help test, you get free access forever, guaranteed. Down the road there may be a freemium version, but testers are locked in.

What math facts does it cover?

Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of 1-2 digit numbers. The four games also cover number properties like multiples, factors, primes, even/odd, and more. We're adding more operations over time.

How is my child's data handled?

Zero data is collected or transmitted. Everything — progress, scores, dragon names, settings — is stored on the device only. No accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ad SDKs. Read our full privacy policy.

How is this different from other math apps?

Most math apps are drill-and-practice with a coat of paint. Math Dragons has four genuinely different games, a dragon companion that evolves with your child's progress, adaptive difficulty that focuses on weak spots, and zero monetization tricks. It was built for one kid first — not for investors.

Questions or feedback?

Bug report, feature request, or just want to say hi — we'd love to hear from you.
